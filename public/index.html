Percentages are of your test's power (watts), not its time or speed. Watts use the Concept2 formula, 2.80 ÷ (split/500)³. Zones are your 2k split plus seconds, from ErgManiac's zone guide. Without a saved 2k, the 2k is estimated with Paul's Law (+5 s/500m each time distance doubles). Your tests are saved on this device only. Pace Card is not affiliated with Concept2 or ErgManiac.
